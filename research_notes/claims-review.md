# Adversarial claims review

Checked 3 October 2026 against the claim list, all six research notes, and the nose-cleanser report. The named science papers exist; that does not validate Element's measurements or tuning. **SUPPORTED** means the source supports the stated, bounded finding. **UNSUPPORTED** includes figures that could not be independently retrieved, not necessarily figures proved false. Prices are page snapshots, not checkout quotes.

## Five claims most exposed in judge Q&A

1. **“Where is evidence that your skin test improves the perfume?”** Answer: “The science motivates exploration; it does not validate our kit. We plan to check measurement agreement and run blind comparisons against quiz-only recommendations.” Do not promise genetic profiling, clinical thresholds, or proven optimization.
2. **“Is the HK$2bn market and 91% adoption figure reliable?”** Answer: “The market total is not independently verified, so we should omit it. The adoption figure belongs to an issuer-reported HK–Macau survey, with unknown representativeness. Our demand case needs pilot purchases and conversion data, not demographic multiplication.”
3. **“Are you really the first or only provider?”** Answer: “We have not established that. Workshops, custom blends and quiz-selected discovery kits already exist. Our proposed difference is an at-home measurement and feedback workflow; exact-match competitors may exist outside the reviewed sources.”
4. **“Have you proved coffee fails and the dish resets smell?”** Answer: “One exploratory identification study found no coffee advantage; lemon's exact statistics remain unverified. The dish is a pause cue, with no demonstrated cleansing action. Recovery varies, so a timed pause is not a guarantee of baseline sensitivity.”
5. **“Can you profitably fulfill a full-credit, alcohol-based kit?”** Answer: “Costs remain estimates and carrier acceptance remains unresolved. We must quote actual supplies, confirm the exact shipment and calculate cohort contribution after kit costs, credits, shipping and acquisition. Pickup is a fallback; the credit alone does not prove viable economics.”

## Internal contradictions and unresolved assumptions

- **Lemon statistics:** `science_adaptation_and_cleansers.md`, Q2, acknowledges the secondary source's P < .05/non-effect conflict. `industry_practice.md`, section 2, declares the differences nonsignificant. The report's legal section further says both coffee and lemon were no better than air; the verified abstract only establishes the coffee comparison.
- **Own-skin evidence:** science Q3 says no direct efficacy test; industry guidance and the report strengthen practice recommendations into scientific support for a neutral reference. Familiar body odor is not proof of effective reset or absence of soap/perfume residue.
- **Protocol/timing:** notes alternate between 30–60 seconds, 2–3 minutes and 30 seconds–3 minutes. Some add 20–30 seconds of rest after a triplet, while the cited method presents a triplet every 20 seconds. The source's staircase reversals and three alternatives also differ from a simple ascending/two-vial kit ladder.
- **Vessel reuse/safety:** practicality section 6 says a sniff cup can double as a strip holder; the report explains that used strips destroy neutrality. The report claims “no safety or regulatory burden” and then requires glass-breakage precautions and a Cap. 456 review.
- **Price positioning:** competitors section 1 says a HK$150–250 kit is below every niche set, despite listing Nette/Etat Libre around HK$220–240. The proposed Discovery/Lab/bottle prices also differ across competitors (HK$148–228/480–780/780–1,180) and unit economics (HK$60–120/250–450/350–700); these are unsettled scenarios.
- **AI budget:** unit economics section 6 claims under HK$4, then budgets HK$1–5 including retries/storage. Its old image-token heuristic is not a verified budget for every current model.
- **Shipping:** aggregators' liquid permissions, air dangerous-goods restrictions and intra-HK delivery are treated as interchangeable. Those sources do not establish one consistent local acceptance rule.
