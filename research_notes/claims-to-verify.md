## Science (business pitch doc, cited from memory, not yet checked)
1. Sight, hearing and touch are relayed through the thalamus before cortex; smell goes almost directly to olfactory cortex, amygdala and hippocampal region (secondary thalamic route exists). Courtiol & Wilson 2015, Front Neural Circuits 9:49.
2. Odour-cued memory of a meaningful perfume produced more amygdala activation than visual cue. Herz, Eliassen, Beland, Souza 2004, Neuropsychologia 42(3):371-378.
3. Odour-evoked autobiographical memories come from earlier in life (first decade) than word/picture cued memories. Willander & Larsson 2006, Psychonomic Bulletin & Review 13(2):240-244.
4. Men given a fragranced product felt more confident; women watching silent videos rated them more attractive. Roberts et al. 2009, Int J Cosmet Sci 31(1):47-54.
5. Humans have ~400 smell receptor types; two people differ substantially in receptor function. Mainland et al. 2014, Nat Neurosci 17:114-120.
6. Whether androstenone smells sweet, foul or of nothing depends largely on one receptor gene (OR7D4). Keller et al. 2007, Nature 449:468-472.
7. Healthy skin surface pH ~4.5-5.5, natural average below 5. Lambers et al. 2006, Int J Cosmet Sci 28(5).
8. Purified water exposed to air drifts to about pH 5.6 within minutes (CO2 absorption).
9. Sniffin' Sticks threshold test (Hummel et al. 1997, Chem Senses 22(1)): ascending, blinded, timed.
10. Specific anosmias exist, including to some musks.
