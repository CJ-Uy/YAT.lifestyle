# Hong Kong Fragrance Market: Size, Demographics and Trends (for a HK-only personalized perfume test kit)

Research date: 2026-10-03. Source quality is marked inline: [PRIMARY] = government or the company's own release; [PAYWALLED] = only a summary or snippet was visible; [LOW] = vendor blog or SEO content with no cited methodology; [SNIPPET] = number taken from a search-engine summary and not checked against the full page.

## 1. How big is the Hong Kong fragrance market, and is it growing?

### Takeaway
Hong Kong's fragrance market is small but real and growing slowly. The best visible estimate is about €240m (about US$260m, about HK$2bn) in 2024, growing about 2% a year. Premium/luxury brands make up most of it, and Euromonitor (May 2026) says premium fragrances drive value growth while retail becomes more experiential. All reliable market-size figures are paywalled, so treat the totals as order-of-magnitude.

### Cited Findings
- Statista Market Insights, "Fragrances – Hong Kong": revenue projected at **€240.00m in 2024**, **CAGR 2.18% (2024–2029)**, **56% of 2024 sales from Luxury**. [PAYWALLED][SNIPPET] This came from an older, proxied copy of the Statista page. On the live page every figure is masked ("US$******m") and needs a paid account. Statista says the model is "updated twice a year", so current figures may differ. — [Statista outlook (paywalled)](https://www.statista.com/outlook/cmo/beauty-personal-care/fragrances/hong-kong); snippet via [proxied copy](https://fr-statista-com.ezproxy.canberra.edu.au/outlook/cmo/beauty-personal-care/fragrances/hong-kong)
- Statista: revenue of Hong Kong's *fragrance product distribution industry* was **over HK$1.12bn in 2019**, forecast at **HK$1.42bn in 2022**. [PAYWALLED][SNIPPET] This measures the distribution (wholesale) industry, not consumer retail spending. — [Statista via proxy](https://www-statista-com.ezproxy.canberra.edu.au/statistics/1031602/hong-kong-fragrance-product-distribution-industry-market-size)
- Euromonitor, "Fragrances in Hong Kong, China" (published **May 2026**, US$1,195). From the public summary: Estée Lauder HK held a **14% retail value share in 2025, down from 17% in 2020**, and "premium brands face rising competition from regional entrants driving fragmentation." Premium fragrances lead value growth. The summary also says "fragrance retail as a lifestyle experience", gamified retail and wellness/stress-relief scents (Adidas Vibes) are shaping demand, offline retail dominates through experiential shopping, and e-commerce is growing for discovery. Outlook runs to 2030. No HKD size appears in the free summary. [PAYWALLED] — [Euromonitor report page](https://www.euromonitor.com/fragrances-in-hong-kong-china/report)
- Mintel and IndexBox both publish HK fragrance market-size products ("Fragrances – Hong Kong, China (2023)"; "Hong Kong SAR – Perfumes and Toilet Waters"). No figures were visible. [PAYWALLED] — [Mintel snapshot](https://marketsizesbeta.mintel.com/snapshots/HKG/526); [IndexBox](https://indexbox.co.uk/store/hong-kong-sar-china-perfumes-and-toilet-waters-market-analysis-forecast-size-trends-and-insights/)
- HK Census & Statistics Dept retail data: sales in the broad **"medicines and cosmetics"** category **rose 4.2% in 2025 as a whole**, with monthly year-on-year gains of +8.7% (May 2025), +6.0% (June), +5% (August) and +9.2% (November 2025). [PRIMARY data reported by press][SNIPPET] This category includes all cosmetics and medicines, not only fragrance, and C&SD has no separate fragrance line. — [Marketing-Interactive, Dec 2025 retail](https://www.marketing-interactive.com/hk-retail-sales-rise-6-6-yoy-in-december-2025); [The Standard, Nov 2025](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/news/article/320651/Hong-Kong-retail-sales-rise-65pc-in-November-2025); [C&SD press release PDF, 3 Feb 2026](https://gia.info.gov.hk/general/202602/03/P2026020300456_531276_1_1770103979194.pdf) (PDF did not render for extraction)
- Context from Mainland China, from Eternal Beauty's 2025 white paper: the China fragrance market grew from **RMB11.4bn (2018) to RMB22.9bn (2023)** and is projected at **RMB44bn by 2028**. China's perfume penetration is reported as **under 5%**, with a growing base of Gen Z enthusiasts. — [Eternal Beauty white paper release via iTiger, 14 Aug 2025](https://www.itiger.com/news/1114286246); [Premium Beauty News, niche perfumes / Chinese brands](https://www.premiumbeautynews.com/en/niche-perfumes-chinese-brands,25671)

### Inferences
- In pitch terms, Hong Kong is a **mature, premium-led market of roughly US$250–300m a year** with low single-digit growth. The opportunity is in *how* people buy (discovery, experience, personalization), not in a fast-growing total. This matches Euromonitor's framing of "experience" and "fragmentation".
- A fragmented market where the leading company's share is falling (17% to 14%) is friendlier to new or indie entrants.
- If 56% of spend is luxury, average bottle prices are high, so a first purchase is a high-regret decision. That supports the case for a low-cost test kit before committing to a full bottle.

### Gaps
- No free, current (2025/2026) HKD figure for HK fragrance retail sales. Euromonitor, Statista, Mintel and IndexBox are all paywalled. Mordor, IMARC and Grand View do not appear to publish a HK-specific fragrance report (none found).
- C&SD does not separate fragrance from "medicines and cosmetics". The absolute HKD value of that category in 2025 could not be pulled because the PDF did not render.
- How much HK fragrance spending comes from tourists versus residents is unknown. This matters because the kit targets locals.

## 2. Hong Kong population aged 18–34, and per-capita spending

### Takeaway
Hong Kong has about **1.17 million people aged 20–34** and about **1.46 million aged 15–34** (provisional, mid-2026), out of 7.52 million. The 25–34 group is much larger than 15–24, so the core 18–30 "first real perfume" group is roughly 0.9–1.0 million people (estimate).

### Cited Findings
- C&SD Table 110-01001, "Population by sex and age group" (thousands; pulled from the official C&SD API on 2026-10-03) [PRIMARY]:

  | Age | End-2025 | Mid-2026 (provisional) | Share of total |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | 15–19 | 288.8k | 288.7k | 3.8% |
  | 20–24 | 295.2k | 295.3k | 3.9% |
  | 25–29 | 380.4k | 376.4k | 5.0–5.1% |
  | 30–34 | 496.6k | 495.8k | 6.6% |
  | **Total population** | 7,508.7k | 7,518.3k | 100% |

  — [C&SD Web Table 110-01001](https://www.censtatd.gov.hk/en/web_table.html?id=110-01001) (API: `https://www.censtatd.gov.hk/api/get.php?id=110-01001&lang=en&full_series=1`)
- Mid-2025 total population: **7,527,500**, made up of 7,247,600 Usual Residents and 279,900 Mobile Residents. It was "virtually unchanged" from mid-2024. [PRIMARY] — [news.gov.hk, 14 Aug 2025](https://www.news.gov.hk/eng/2025/08/20250814/20250814_163532_979.html); [info.gov.hk press release](https://www.info.gov.hk/gia/general/202508/14/P2025081400404.htm)
- Statista's HK fragrance outlook includes a per-capita revenue figure, but it is masked on the public page. [PAYWALLED] — [Statista](https://www.statista.com/outlook/cmo/beauty-personal-care/fragrances/hong-kong)

### Inferences
- Rough sizing from the C&SD numbers: ages 18–19 are about 2/5 of the 15–19 group (about 115k, assuming an even age spread). Adding ages 20–29 (about 672k) and age 30 (about 1/5 of 30–34, about 99k) gives **about 0.88m people aged 18–30**. This is my calculation, not an official figure.
- Rough per-capita spending: €240m / 7.5m people ≈ **€32 (about HK$270) per person per year**. This is my calculation, and it includes tourist purchases, so resident spending is likely lower. Label it as an estimate if used.
- A hypothetical kit priced at about HK$200–400 is roughly one year's average per-capita fragrance spend, which is plausible as a gift or a one-time "find my scent" purchase.
- Because the 25–34 group (about 872k) is far larger than 18–24 (about 400k), the first-job / mid-20s group is the largest part of the target.

### Gaps
- No HK-specific per-capita fragrance spending broken down by age.
- C&SD Table 110-01001 includes foreign domestic helpers, most of whom are women aged 25–44. They make the 25–34 numbers look larger than the local young-adult market really is. The table that excludes FDHs (110-01001A) was not extracted, so treat 25–34 as an upper bound.

## 3. Trends: niche, personalization, workshops, sampling, social media, gifting

### Takeaway
There is strong survey evidence that Hong Kong consumers are fragrance-engaged and open to new things: 91.6% have used perfume, over 80% rotate more than one scent, over 90% are open to niche brands, and about 40% research on social media. Hong Kong also has a visible ecosystem of DIY perfume-making workshops, including subsidized university sessions, which shows demand for "make or choose your own scent" experiences. Hard HK data on sampling and gifting is thin, so global data has to fill that gap.

### Cited Findings
**Hong Kong consumer surveys (Eternal Group / Eternal Beauty white papers, launched at Cosmoprof Asia). These are industry and company surveys, not independent ones: Eternal is a HK fragrance distributor.**
- 2024 white paper (released **18 Nov 2024**, **n = 1,063** HK and Macau respondents):
  - **91.56%** use perfume.
  - **Over 80%** use more than one perfume.
  - **60.46%** pick a scent by mood, occasion or season; 21.57% have one daily and one special-occasion scent; 17.97% use only one.
  - Scent preferences: floral **48.19%**, woody 21%, fruity 14.95%, citrus 11.21%.
  - **Close to 40%** use social media for fragrance information.
  - **Over 30%** prefer buying in brand physical stores; **20%** buy on online platforms.
  - **Over 90%** are open to trying niche brands.
  - Demand for unisex scents is rising.
  - No age breakdown was published in the release.

  — [PR Newswire, Eternal Group 2024 White Paper](https://prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/eternal-group-unveiled-the-2024-hong-kong-and-macau-perfume-and-home-fragrance-white-paper-at-cosmoprof-asia-2024-302308495.html)
- 2025 white paper (released **14 Aug 2025**, **n = 790** HK and Macau respondents):
  - **81%** have made fragrance part of daily life, **up 9 percentage points** year on year.
  - **86%** extend their personal scent preferences to their homes.
  - **70%** prefer shopping spaces that use fragrance design.
  - Fragrance makes **58%** shop longer and raises purchase intention for **52%**.
  - Emotional roles: relaxation (30%), joy and comfort (26.8%), tranquility (22%), stress and anxiety relief (almost 18%).

  — [iTiger / Eternal Beauty (06883) release](https://www.itiger.com/news/1114286246)

**Euromonitor HK (May 2026):** shift toward "holistic scent management for daily wellbeing". Consumers want "products and experiences that align with their lifestyles". Gamified retail strengthens engagement. "Cultural storytelling" (e.g. To Summer's HK debut) sets brands apart. [PAYWALLED summary] — [Euromonitor](https://www.euromonitor.com/fragrances-in-hong-kong-china/report)

**Perfume-making workshops in Hong Kong (shows demand for personalization as an experience):**
- Yues.Studio (founded 2020) says it has taught **more than 2,000 students** and runs tailor-made perfume workshops for companies, brands and NGOs. PerfumeTalk (perfumer Ryan Kwong) has run for about 7 years. Intime Artisan de Parfum runs French-style workshops in Tsim Sha Tsui. The Standard groups perfume-making with other "healing" craft workshops that have "emerged in recent years". — [The Standard, "3 Hong Kong art workshops…"](https://www.thestandard.com.hk/wellness/article/336373/3-Hong-Kong-art-workshops-for-a-creative-soulful-escape); [Time Out HK – Intime](https://www.timeout.com/hong-kong/shopping/intime-artisan-de-parfum)
- Universities and colleges run them for students:
  - HKUST Dean of Students' Office: fully subsidized "Scent-sational Workshop: DIY your Perfume". — [HKUST calendar](https://calendar.hkust.edu.hk/zh-hans/events/scent-sational-workshop-diy-your-perfume)
  - HKU SPACE: "Handmade Perfume Experiential Workshop – Create your Signature Scent" (6 Sep 2025). — [HKU SPACE](https://hkuspace.hku.hk/event/20250906/handmade-perfume-experiential-workshop-create-your-signature-scent)
  - VTC "The Perfume Studio" course (June 2026 flyer): accord families, 33 accords, take home a 20ml self-made perfume, **HK$280**. — [VTC PDF](https://hospitality.vtc.edu.hk/cms/sites/default/files/2026-06/The%20Perfume%20Studio%201.pdf)
  - HKMU and PolyU have also hosted them. — [HKMU](https://www.hkmu.edu.hk/sol/tc/?p=54649); [PolyU CIHK](https://www.polyu.edu.hk/cihk/news-and-events/news/2023/workshop-(3)/)
- Workshops also appear on Time Auction (volunteer-reward experiences, Yues Studio 2023 Q4), Pinkoi, Airbnb Experiences, and Little Steps Asia (parents and kids). — [Time Auction](https://timeauction.org/en/experience_products/463-perfume-making-workshop-at-yues-studio-2023-q4); [Pinkoi](https://en.pinkoi.com/product/6ghFPkcd); [Little Steps Asia](https://www.littlestepsasia.com/hong-kong/shop/shops-for-parents/perfume-making-workshops/)
- Gifting and couples: HK01's Valentine's guide (published 12 Feb 2024, updated 18 Feb 2025) lists 7 couples' workshops. A scent-related one, a scented bear music box at MINILARGE, Lai Chi Kok, with "choose your own scent" and a couples discount, is included as a "one-of-a-kind" gift. A pure perfume-blending workshop was *not* on that list. — [HK01](https://www.hk01.com/談情說性/989432/情人節好去處2024-7大情侶工作坊-行動表愛意-共同打造甜蜜回憶)

**Niche, Gen Z and social media (mostly global or China evidence):**
- Mintel names Jo Malone, Tom Ford, Maison Margiela, Byredo and Diptyque as top niche brands of 2024. — [Mintel fragrance trends](https://www.mintel.com/beauty-and-personal-care-market-news/make-sense-of-scents-fragrance-industry-trends-in-2024-and-beyond/)
- Gen Z is redefining perfume use (identity, gender-neutral scents, social discovery). — [Premium Beauty News, "Gen Z redefines perfume"](https://premiumbeautynews.com/en/gen-z-redefines-perfume-and-its,25242); [BeautyMatter, evolution of the Chinese fragrance market](https://beautymatter.com/articles/the-evolution-of-the-chinese-fragrance-market)

**Sampling and discovery-set culture (US data; secondary reporting):**
- Circana: fragrance led US beauty dollar growth in Q1 2025 (mass +8%, prestige +4%). Minis and travel sets pushed the gift-set segment up **45% in Q1 2025**. [SNIPPET] — [Beauty Independent, "Hot fragrance category shows signs of cooling"](https://www.beautyindependent.com/hot-fragrance-category-shows-signs-cooling/); [Happi](https://www.happi.com/?p=249551)
- Adobe Digital Insights (as reported): sample and discovery sets were about **27% of fragrance sales** over Thanksgiving to Cyber Monday. YipitData: fragrance was **18% of Sephora's sales** (Jan–Aug) vs 16% a year earlier. [SNIPPET; exact year not verified, likely 2024 or 2025] — [Beauty Independent](https://www.beautyindependent.com/sol-de-janeiro-kayali-sephora-top-fragrances/)

### Inferences
- The behaviours a test kit relies on already exist in Hong Kong: people rotate scents by mood (60%), are open to niche brands (90%+), use social media for discovery (about 40%), and buy in-store (30%+). A kit turns "visit several counters and get overwhelmed" into "try at home, then commit".
- HK perfume workshops cost about HK$280 at the subsidized or college end, and private studios charge more. Universities run them as student-engagement events, which shows young adults have an appetite for "create or choose my signature scent". A take-home kit is a cheaper, repeatable, giftable version of that experience.
- Valentine's guides already present "choose your own scent" items as unique couples' gifts. This supports the gift-buyer angle, though there is no direct HK data on how much fragrance is bought as gifts.

### Gaps
- No HK-specific data on TikTok/Xiaohongshu (小紅書) fragrance trends among HK youth. Only China and global Gen Z commentary was found.
- No HK data on the gift share of fragrance sales or on seasonal peaks (Valentine's, Christmas, graduation). C&SD monthly retail data could show December peaks for "medicines and cosmetics", but it was not extracted.
- The Eternal white papers did not release age breakdowns. Results for 18–30 specifically are unknown.
- No count or attendance figures for the HK perfume-workshop market overall. Yues.Studio's "2,000+ students" is a self-reported single-studio figure.

## 4. Global personalized/custom fragrance market (supporting evidence)

### Takeaway
Reputable firms describe personalization as a *driver* of the global perfume market (about US$50bn, growing about 4–6% a year). However, I found **no credible, citable size figure for the "personalized fragrance" segment** itself. The figures that circulate (€1.13bn or US$1.5bn by 2028) come from low-quality sources. Use the overall market and the "personalization driver" framing, not a segment size.

### Cited Findings
- IMARC: global perfume market to grow at about **5% CAGR 2025–2033**, "catalyzed by growing popularity of personalized products". — [IMARC Global Perfume Market](https://www.imarcgroup.com/global-perfume-market)
- Global Industry Analysts (via GII): global fragrances and perfumes market **US$47.5bn (2023) → US$63.8bn (2030), 4.3% CAGR**. [PAYWALLED summary] — [GII listing](https://cn.gii.tw/report/go1514213-fragrances-perfumes.html)
- Grand View Research (as quoted in search results): global perfume market **US$50.85bn in 2022**, **5.9% CAGR 2023–2030**. [SNIPPET; the Grand View page itself was not fetched]
- "Personalized fragrance market expected to hit **€1.13bn by 2028**" and "niche grows 9.1%/yr vs mass 2.69%". [LOW] This is from Scento, a perfume subscription vendor, with no methodology shown. — [Scento blog](https://www.scento.com/blog/bespoke-perfume-rising)
- "Personalized perfume market projected to reach **$1.5bn by 2028**". [LOW] This is an unattributed SEO blog figure, possibly a USD version of the same number. Do not put it on a slide without a primary source.

### Inferences
- A safe slide line: "Global perfume ≈ US$50–64bn, growing ~4–6%/yr; analysts (IMARC) cite personalization as a key growth driver."

### Gaps
- No primary-source size for the personalized/custom fragrance segment (Grand View, Mordor and Future Market Insights segment pages were not found or not accessible).

## 5. How consumers choose perfume: in-store testing problems and unworn bottles

### Takeaway
The "blind buys, regret, unused bottles" story is widely repeated, but almost every specific number traces back to vendor blogs with no cited source. Only one claim has a named analyst: Mintel says about 30% of shoppers won't buy without testing in person. HK data only shows that in-store is the leading channel (30%+). Present the problem qualitatively, or use the Mintel and HK white-paper figures only.

### Cited Findings
- Mintel senior beauty analyst Clare Hennigan (quoted by Scento): **about 30% of consumers won't buy fragrance without testing it in person**. [Secondary quote of a named analyst; original Mintel source not seen] — [Scento consumer-behavior stats](https://www.scento.com/blog/perfume-consumer-behavior-statistics-2026-how-people-buy-fragrance)
- Scento/RIFM joint survey (Nov 2024–Jan 2025): **12.8 million unused designer/niche bottles in European homes**. [LOW-to-MEDIUM: vendor-run survey with a named partner; methodology not published on the page] — [Scento](https://www.scento.com/blog/perfume-consumer-behavior-statistics-2026-how-people-buy-fragrance)
- Widely repeated figures with **no source cited on the page**, all [LOW], do not use as fact: "67% own at least one unworn bottle / regret a purchase", "4.3 unused bottles per person", "€340 regretted spend over 3 years", "€780m wasted across Europe", "33% of US adults buy without testing", "sampling reduces regret by 86%", "3.2x repurchase with sampling", "78% of Gen Z start with travel sizes". — [Scento](https://www.scento.com/blog/perfume-consumer-behavior-statistics-2026-how-people-buy-fragrance) (the vendor's own table lists "no source" for each)
- Hong Kong: **over 30%** prefer brand physical stores and **20%** online platforms, and **about 40%** get fragrance information from social media. — [Eternal Group 2024 White Paper](https://prnewswire.com/apac/news-releases/eternal-group-unveiled-the-2024-hong-kong-and-macau-perfume-and-home-fragrance-white-paper-at-cosmoprof-asia-2024-302308495.html)
- A blotter or quick wrist spray doesn't show how a scent develops over hours. This is a qualitative, widely accepted point (also made in fragrance-community discussions). — [Basenotes forum thread on sampling](https://basenotes.com/threads/sampling-without-intent-to-purchase.170997/post-811789); [Undina's blog, "Do you blind buy?" (Jan 2024)](https://undina.com/2024/01/13/saturday-question-do-you-blind-buy-perfumes/)

### Inferences
- The pain point (expensive full bottles, too little time to test in store, regret) is real and widely recognised, but **quantified HK evidence is missing**. For a hackathon slide, pair the solid HK data (91.6% use perfume; 60% choose by mood; 90%+ open to niche; 30%+ buy in-store) with a qualitative "testing at a counter is rushed and blotters lie" point. Global US sampling data (minis and travel sets driving 45% gift-set growth; discovery sets about 27% of holiday fragrance sales) supports the try-before-you-buy behaviour.
- **Do not** put the 67% / 4.3 bottles / €780m figures on the slide as fact. They are unsourced.

### Gaps
- No independent survey (YouGov, Mintel, NPD/Circana) on perfume regret or unworn bottles could be verified directly.
- No HK survey on dissatisfaction with in-store testing, sales-staff pressure, or time spent at counters.
- No data on how first-time buyers aged 18–30 in HK choose their first "real" perfume. This is a good thing for the team to cover with its own quick survey (e.g. a 20-person poll of classmates) to put on the slide.
