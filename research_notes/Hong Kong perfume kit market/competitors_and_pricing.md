## 3. Hong Kong custom perfume-making workshops

### Takeaway
HK has a crowded walk-in "make your own perfume" market. Most sessions cost **HK$280 to 680 for 1.5 to 2 hours**, and the customer leaves with a 15 to 50ml EDP. Premium or education-led sessions run HK$1,000 to 1,200, and certificate courses HK$4,800+. Workshops use 20 to 100+ bases and personalize by preference and guidance. None test skin chemistry or use AI, and all require attending a studio.

## 6. What makes Element different from each competitor, and pricing implications

### Takeaway
Element is the only offer found that combines (a) an at-home **skin and nose test with photos**, (b) **AI tuning** of the formula, and (c) **full kit-price credit** toward a personalized bottle, sold in HK. Each competitor has at most one or two of these.

### Cited Findings (comparison anchors, from sections above)
- Brand discovery sets (Le Labo HK$788 parallel, Diptyque HK$1,060+, Jo Malone 5x1.5ml with HK$175 voucher) sell off-the-shelf scents with no testing, no personalization, and partial or no credit.
